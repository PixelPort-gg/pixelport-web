# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Pixel Port is for Apple Silicon Mac owners who already have Windows games in their Steam library and want a simple, trustworthy way to play them without learning Wine or managing compatibility tooling.

## Product Purpose

The website helps Mac gamers verify that a game will run, trust the compatibility evidence, and confidently choose Pixel Port. Success means replacing compatibility uncertainty and fragile manual setup with a clear path from an owned Steam game to a repeatable Mac launch.

## Positioning

Pixel Port turns supported Windows Steam games into repeatable Mac launches with one click, backed by honest compatibility evidence instead of trial-and-error setup.

## Conversion & proof

- On desktop, the primary CTA is to download Pixel Port for Mac. “See what runs” is the secondary path for visitors who are not ready to download.
- On mobile, the primary CTA is “Email me the Mac download link.” The address is used for that delivery, with a separate, unchecked optional consent for product updates. “See what runs” is the secondary CTA.
- The line a visitor should remember after ten seconds is: “Your Mac can play more than you think.”
- Belief ladder:
  1. My Apple Silicon Mac can play more Windows games than I assumed.
  2. Pixel Port shows honestly which games work, backed by real launch evidence.
  3. It uses the Steam games I already own.
  4. It handles the fragile runtime and launch setup automatically.
  5. I can download it with confidence and get playing quickly.
- Proof on hand includes the live game catalogue, compatibility grades, launch totals, and user totals already represented in the repository. The independent [MacHow2 review](https://machow2.com/pixel-port-mac-review/) supports Pixel Port’s unusually simple, low-configuration setup while also documenting its beta-stage limitations and compatibility caveats.

## Brand Personality

Honest, capable, and effortless. The experience should replace uncertainty with confidence and relief. There are no named visual references to emulate.

## Anti-references

Pixel Port should not resemble a terminal-heavy compatibility tool, a generic neon gamer site, or an overpromising utility that hides caveats.

## Design Principles

- Lead with honest compatibility evidence before making promises.
- Make technical complexity disappear without hiding important limitations.
- Give each visitor a useful next step on the device they are using.
- Prove capability with real games and real launch data.
- Keep the path from curiosity to play calm, direct, and repeatable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve the existing baseline of keyboard access, reduced-motion support, high-contrast accommodation, readable color contrast, and compatibility states that do not rely on color alone. No additional accessibility requirements are currently specified.
